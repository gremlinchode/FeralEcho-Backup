## 1. Decision and scientific claim

**SUPPORTED INFERENCE:** the cheapest credible next experiment is a paired comparison of a competent deterministic proposer with and without an inert, investigator-authored repair record, using exactly the same task authority, bounded analysis, edit vocabulary, verifier and resources. Retain VSL as historical storage/lifecycle infrastructure; do not build a general semantic analyzer or integrate an authority layer into production yet.

The estimand is **the incremental value of access to supplied repair knowledge under a fixed search budget**. It is not the value of verification, which both arms receive; not autonomous acquisition; and not natural-workload retained net utility. An advantage could come from manually supplied information or search ordering. It would not by itself show that a structured record is superior to a small equally informed hand-coded rule. That representation-versus-information distinction is not identifiable from a record/no-record comparison alone.

**UNTESTED HYPOTHESIS:** richer records improve useful proposal selection enough to pay their engineering cost. The experiment is deliberately allowed to reject that hypothesis. If the cheap arm ties or wins on completed repairs, keep the cheap architecture. A safe but less useful record system does not pass. A small efficiency gain does not override a correctness loss or a tied primary outcome.

**BLOCKED / UNKNOWN:** independent case custody, named task/oracle reviewers and their access controls have not been established. New proposer isolation, the observation adapter and shared controller are not implemented or qualified. These are explicit prerequisites, not evidence that an experiment failed. Human approval is required before any implementation. After implementation, a separate execution freeze and qualification are required before scored evaluation.

Evidence labels throughout: **DIRECTLY VERIFIED** means source/bytes or existing raw data checked, not newly executed behavior; **SUPPORTED INFERENCE** means a mechanism-supported conclusion; **UNTESTED HYPOTHESIS** means prospective behavior; **BLOCKED / UNKNOWN** means missing evidence or prerequisites. Sections describing future components are **proposed requirements**, not statements that those components exist.

## 3. Authoritative current-task contract

**Proposed authority model:** for this synthetic engineering screen, requirements originate with the independent task custodian acting as task owner, under the reviewed task-family specification. A separate oracle reviewer checks the translation from that specification to executable expectations. A named human approves the specification and both attestations before scoring. For future real tasks, origin must instead be a versioned owner-approved specification or an independently authoritative test/specification source. A retained skill, model-generated paraphrase, majority vote or successful test run cannot establish missing intent.

Maintain two separate objects:

1. **Current-task requirements:** what this task must do, supplied identically to B/C.
2. **Repair hypothesis:** conditions under which an old edit may be useful, supplied only to C in this screen. It can narrow proposal selection; it cannot change task requirements or acceptance policy.

Every authority envelope binds task ID, task revision, original source hash, entry point, requirements hash, interpretation/observation version, input-domain version and owner/reviewer attestations. A run manifest pins the expected revision; wall-clock age alone does not determine freshness. Changed source, task revision or interpretation invalidates any prior acceptance decision. For a proposed patch, the controller explicitly carries the original authorization context forward to the committed derived-source hash; the proposer cannot rebind the task itself.

Attestations are independently controlled approval records, not proposer-supplied strings. Their allowed issuer and hash are pinned by the custodian's read-only run manifest. Hashes establish integrity, not truth or personal identity. OS custody and a human-reviewed provenance chain establish who approved them. No new public-key infrastructure is necessary for this local screen, but an untrusted JSON field saying `approved=true` is never sufficient.

| State | Detection and mandatory controller response |
|---|---|
| `VALID_CONTRACT` | Recognized reviewed authority; current task/source/revision bound; required fields complete; supported observations; no conflicts. May proceed to analysis. It is still conditional on authority/oracle correctness. |
| `UNKNOWN_CONTRACT` | Required intent, tie policy, type, ownership/effect or authority evidence missing. Explicit unknown, no defaults from Skill A. Retain original, request clarification outside the trial; no proposal execution. |
| `CONFLICTING_CONTRACT` | Two active authority claims disagree; ordered-priority constraints cycle; contradictory effect/type/return requirements; structured envelope disagrees with its approved requirement hash. No “latest wins” or skill-selected precedence. Abstain until owner issues a new revision. |
| `STALE_CONTRACT` | Task/source/revision/interpretation mismatch or superseded attestation in the pinned registry. Refuse; do not silently refresh or accept a record from a related task. |
| `UNSUPPORTED_CONTRACT` | Requirement is understood but outside declared observations/grammar, or issuer/schema cannot be authenticated within the registry. Refuse with unsupported reason; no weaker replacement contract. |

Collect all reason flags. For a single display status use conflict, stale, unsupported, unknown, valid in that order; all non-valid states prohibit repair. Syntax/schema errors are unsupported, not permission to infer omitted facts. Incorrect but internally consistent owner requirements may evade these checks: **SUPPORTED INFERENCE**, no controller can establish unavailable true intent. Independent review and an explicit scope disclaimer reduce this risk; they do not eliminate it.

For this screen, unstructured natural-language contradictions are not machine-resolved. The human-approved structured translation and its source text are linked before execution. If reviewers cannot reconcile them, status is unknown/conflicting and the case is not silently relabeled repairable. Inferred requirements can become authoritative only through this external approval/revision procedure, never automatically during a trial.

## 4. Bounded observation and supported language

**Proposed correctness claim:** a committed one-site edit satisfied reviewed ranking/output/final-value-preservation obligations on a fixed, sampled set of inputs, under a qualified restricted-program checker and isolated evaluator. This is empirical conformance, not arbitrary Python correctness, universal safety or production authority.

Task family: choose a winning name from a nonempty ordinary list of 1–3 entries `[name, score, tag]`. Names are distinct strings; scores are exact integers (bool excluded); tags belong to a declared set of three strings. Current contract specifies score direction, complete tag order, lexicographic name tie-break direction, exact string return, and input preservation. Opposite contracts can legitimately require opposite decisions for identical source. The public task schema supplies field meanings to both arms; variable spellings are not semantic facts.

Per task the custodian commits eight opaque ASCII names, five distinct small integer score values and three tags. All ordered lists of length 1–3 with distinct names drawn from these sets define a finite domain. Only 48 stratified inputs are observed; it is **not exhaustively enumerated**, and no claim extends to arbitrary strings, integers, lengths or task families. The public domain contains no role-signaling names. Exact realized test inputs and outputs remain controller/custodian-only.

Each decision/hidden partition contains 24 disjoint inputs: three singleton; twelve tag-pair cases counterbalancing input order and lexical winner; three score-dominance; three name tie-break; three three-entry cases. Across the partitions vary score extremes, equal scores, equal tags, and triple permutations. The custodian's reviewed sampler must meet these coverage constraints without consulting B/C outcomes. Insufficient distinct inputs or inconsistent oracle expectations stop before scored execution; do not relax coverage. Sampling seeds and exact lists are sealed after implementation freeze, before any arm runs.

The proposed **closed grammar** is intentionally small: one entry function; at most two pure, acyclic local helpers; straight-line assignments, return, lambda/named key; literal maps from strings to distinct integers; tuple/list construction; constant-index reads; unary numeric sign; known `sorted`, `list`, `tuple`, `len`; owned-list `.sort`; and a single-level comprehension over the input without condition or side effect. Known first/last selection and literal sort direction are supported. No import, reflection, attribute access except qualified `.sort`, arbitrary call, recursion, control-flow branch, exception handler, loop beyond that comprehension, decorator, global/nonlocal write, subscript write, dynamic dispatch, rebinding of builtins/helper symbols, or mutable module state. Cap source at 8 KiB, 256 AST nodes, eight editable sign sites and four sorting operations; excess is unsupported, never truncated into a favorable subset.

Binding analysis is limited to lexical scope, unique reaching definitions in straight-line code, fixed field indices, helper parameter substitution and a def-use path to the return. Literal map orientation is a fact extracted from source relative to the *current* contract. A shallow copy owns its outer list only. Sorting that copy is allowed; changing shared inner entries is prohibited. A variable aliased to the input does not own it. Conditional aliases and unresolved ownership fail closed. This is a proposed bounded checker, not an existing sound analyzer.

| Obligation | Measurement or restriction; qualification status |
|---|---|
| Return value and exact type | Observer emits a flat tagged encoding, e.g. string type plus its value. Any non-string return fails, even if Python equality would accept it. New adapter needs qualification. |
| Numeric/type preservation | Observer emits canonical tagged encodings of the complete before/after primitive input tree, with integer payloads as decimal text; `BOOL`, `INT`, `FLOAT` are distinct. No float tolerance, NaN, infinity or implicit coercion. This prevents E0.1's int/float equality from defining the claim. |
| Exceptions/error behavior | Valid-domain tasks require normal return. Candidate exceptions, malformed output and timeouts are unsolved outcomes, not successful abstentions. Exact exception-message or recovery semantics are outside scope. |
| Input mutation | Compare final complete typed values; additionally reject every input/inner-entry write and every `.sort` whose outer-list ownership is unproved. Final equality alone does not prove no temporary mutation. |
| Identity and aliases | No identity-sensitive tasks or aliased input graph: fresh JSON trees only. Local input aliases must be tracked to prohibit mutation. No claim of general alias/identity preservation across a serialization boundary. Stronger identity requirements are unsupported. |
| Local/global effects | Local allocation and owned outer-list sorting allowed. All other mutation, I/O, ambient state access and dynamic effects rejected by grammar; OS confinement remains a backstop. No claim that E0.1 observes arbitrary side effects. |
| Multiple sites and helpers | All sites enumerated with AST paths, unique definitions and return dependencies; dead sites cannot authorize edits. Unknown bindings or forged claims are rejected by recomputation, not accepted as proof. |
| Shadowing and unsupported structures | Validate before execution; no fallback to unrestricted execution. A task requiring unsupported structure is an unsupported outcome, not a secretly missing positive. |
| Infrastructure | Launch/profile failure, observer inconsistency, write/isolation failure or missing durable evidence stops the pass. Child timeout alone is a task outcome unless boundary diagnosis establishes infrastructure failure. |

The wrapper is not a second trusted process inside the child. Its observations remain untrusted until compared by E0.1; restricted source validation, reserved observer names and prohibition of reflection are necessary to make its limited observation credible. This new wrapper/checker combination needs fresh adversarial qualification. Do not claim unchanged E0.1 source alone qualifies it.

## 6. Decisive cheap-baseline comparison

**Arm A — historical reference:** reuse existing Skill A evidence descriptively. Do not execute a third arm in the principal screen. A has a different architecture/verifier and does not identify the record effect. Optional future descriptive reproduction needs separate authorization, not extra calls concealed in this budget.

**Arm B — generic verified proposer:** deterministic one-site numeric-sign toggles over the supported AST. Use the same bounded binding/effect analysis as C; eliminate nonnumeric, dead, unsafe and unresolvable sites. The edit universe contains adding/removing a unary minus around a numeric key component or its uniquely defined extracted expression. Both directions are available; do not restrict B to Skill A's historical variable names, exact AST or `sorted` context. Deduplicate equivalent patch locations and preserve all eligible sites before ranking.

B ranks live ranking-key sites before other return-dependent numeric sites, then map-derived key components before direct numeric fields, then shortest def-use distance to the returned selection, then source-position order. These are disclosed generic engineering heuristics, not learned competence. B shares alpha/helper normalization, ownership checks and task facts with C; it may exploit all of them. It must enumerate all legal options when there are at most two. It may not return an empty proposal list merely because no skill exists. It has no hand-coded historic task IDs, target variable names, source hashes or answer tables.

**Arm C — semantic-record verified proposer:** same executable, candidate universe and generic fallback. A fixed inert-record interpreter can prioritize eligible sites when a record's role predicates and polarity relation hold. It may put a hypothesized safe-direction edit ahead of generic ordering, then fills unused slots from B's order. Unknown record predicates do not create authority; unsupported or contradictory record data yields a recorded abstention/error, never an unreviewed new operator. The record cannot add tests, increase budgets, access labels or waive shared eligibility checks.

The proposed record contributes **cross-task repair knowledge**: a relation between sorting/selection direction, numeric rank orientation and sign; operand-role constraints; known failure contexts; and applicability limitations. Current tag order, ownership policy, live binding facts and expected values do **not** come from it. Its provenance is `INVESTIGATOR_AUTHORED`, distinct from historical Skill A and future acquired records. Its concrete contents must be frozen with the implementation before held-out instantiation; this mission does not author the executable record.

Both receive identical authoritative task facts and shared extracted facts. The extra information is the record's mapping from those facts to a repair priority. B is not prohibited from having an ordinary generic heuristic that happens to derive the same decision. An independent implementer/reviewer must approve B's competence on disclosed development material before freezing either arm. If a cheap polarity-consistency check naturally belongs in that baseline, include and freeze it for both; do not remove a competent baseline feature to make C win. Such a pre-freeze change must be recorded in the implementation specification, never chosen after holdout results.

**Fairness limit:** any C win estimates information-plus-selection value, not an intrinsic representational advantage. A simple flat priority rule could encode the same relation. Before paying for a richer architecture, compare the actual code/maintenance cost of that rule with the inert-record interpreter, without testing a revised baseline on this holdout. If they are essentially the same rule in different locations, say so. A subsequent equally informed representation comparison would need its own fresh design; it is not silently added here.

At most two proposals/arm/unit, committed simultaneously with no feedback. Same two proposals consume the same checks regardless of arm. Candidate capacity is not padded with deliberate irrelevant sites; case selection must not inspect B/C rankings. Different proposer choices are the treatment effect, not a matching failure. Any candidate universe or common analyzer discrepancy between arms is an integrity failure.

### Shared acceptance procedure

1. Validate current task authority and source subset. Invalid/missing authority ⇒ abstain without inventing correctness labels. Unsupported code ⇒ retain original, record unsupported.
2. Recompute each proposed binding, exact single-site edit, scope and ownership from original source. Reject forged, dead, ambiguous or disallowed edits. Record failed attempts against the budget.
3. Evaluate original on all 24 decision inputs. If it satisfies all obligations, do not accept an edit. If it fails, inspect committed proposals in order, with no new proposer contact.
4. For a proposal require complete decision-suite conformance, at least one independently observed original failure corrected, and no new failure. Stop proposal evaluation at its first failed input to save cost; preserve partial results. Accept the first passing eligible proposal; otherwise retain original. No partial repair or irrelevant edit is a completed task.
5. Seal both output hashes/decisions. Independently score original and final outputs on 24 hidden inputs. Hidden failures cannot cause a different edit, retry, contract revision or additional proposal. Record original-versus-final regressions and correctness on decision plus hidden observations.

A shared verifier cannot give different verdicts on identical `(source, contract, evidence, checker)` inputs based on record availability. C cannot “reject an unsafe proposal B accepts” through a private verifier. Any observed difference must come from different committed proposals/order/abstention, or else indicate an implementation defect. Harmful unproposed alternatives are not credited as measured prevented harms without corresponding B evidence.

## 7. Independent custody and qualification design

**BLOCKED / UNKNOWN now:** no independent custodian or oracle reviewer has accepted these roles. A second agent in the same readable workspace is not a secrecy boundary. This assistant has seen historical generators and must not implement against undisclosed claims of blindness to them.

| Role | Responsibility and separation |
|---|---|
| Architecture designer | Publishes grammar, factors, comparison, interfaces and thresholds; no future scored-case access. May also implement, with that overlap disclosed. |
| Implementer | Uses only disclosed historical/development material; freezes both arms, common verifier interface and engineered record before case instantiation. Cannot read custody storage. |
| Independent case custodian | Independently selects semantic recombinations after the freeze; stores sources/labels/witnesses/seed/answers under a separate OS identity or offline machine outside implementer tool access. No implementation edits. |
| Oracle reviewer | Checks owner intent and oracle through a separately written direct pairwise specification plus manual invariant review; must not merely reuse proposer rank-map/sign logic. Reviews without B/C outcomes. May not self-certify an oracle they alone authored. |
| Final evidence reviewer | Obtains sealed implementation/protocol/case manifests, complete journal and unblinded mapping after run; verifies paired inputs, all outcomes, independence and interpretation. |

Named humans and/or controlled processes must fill these roles. Process independence can enforce information flow, but a single author creating implementation and hidden answers is not epistemically independent; disclose such overlap and restrict claims to disclosed engineering checks. **That fallback does not satisfy the proposed independent Gate 2.** No relay or paid service is required; an offline custodian is sufficient. Confirm availability/access before authorizing implementation to avoid building an experiment that cannot be independently judged.

Sequence: human approves this design and role/access plan → implementation and disclosed component qualification → freeze all engine/record hashes and the independently reviewed oracle implementation/specification → custodian independently instantiates cases under the approved procedure → owner/oracle validation and sealed manifest → single scored run → full release to evidence reviewer. The oracle is frozen before case outcomes, not adjusted to accommodate a witness. The implementer may receive only a readiness/abort notice during preflight, not per-case evidence. No held-out repair labels, witness edits or generated answers are included in this design.

### Small screen: sixteen held units, not sixteen independent population samples

Retain 8 repairable + 8 refusal units. This is enough for a **costly-engineering go/no-go screen**, not a reliability estimate or natural-workload test. Cases intentionally stress known mechanisms; do not call them randomly sampled future Echo work. Counterfactual pairs and shared templates reduce independence; use descriptive paired counts, no p-value or deployment error-rate estimate.

Positive strata: two units each for (i) alpha-renamed/direct equivalents, (ii) extracted expressions/pure helpers, (iii) equivalent `sorted`/owned `.sort` arrangements, (iv) multiple live/dead candidate sites. Every positive must be independently completely repairable by an allowed single-site edit; the witness is custodian-only. Each stratum must contain a new combination of at least two relevant semantic factors, not merely new bytes or names.

Negative/refusal strata: one already-correct opposite-contract twin of a positive; one irrelevant/dead apparent repair; one alias/ownership violation; one unsupported builtin/helper shadow; one missing required fact; one contradictory contract; one stale binding/revision; one incomplete repair with another independent obligation still failing. Incorrect binding claims are additionally attacked in component qualification in both rooms, not counted as extra independent task samples. Harmful neighboring sign changes and counterbalanced live-site positions are included across the program strata.

Custodian uses factor sampling, not a published sixteen-source recipe: grammar production, alpha names, map orientation, sort direction, first/last selection, helper/temporary placement and edit-site positions drawn from declared finite choices. Balance `sorted`/owned-sort and orientation/selection combinations across positives; in multi-site cases balance the live edit before/after the distractor. Balance site counts over 1, 2 and 3–4 eligible sign sites without screening on either proposer's preferred position. Do not add distractors just to exhaust B's two proposals. Cases outside these constraints are ineligible before any arm sees them.

The custodian precommits a private seed and candidate-pool manifest, then selects the first qualifying unit per stratum in seeded order, at most two instantiations per slot. Eligibility uses only grammar, authoritative oracle and witness validation; never B/C outcomes. Preserve every rejected instantiation and reason. A slot still unqualified after two is a pretrial design failure; stop rather than searching indefinitely for convenient cases. No replacement after scoring starts. Exact duplicate source/contract pairs are excluded, except the intended same-source/different-contract twin. The custodian compares hashes to historical sources without exposing them to the proposer; semantic novelty is reviewed separately, not inferred from hash inequality.

Oracle validation uses original/witness results under isolated execution **only after future authorization**. Validate positives across both input partitions; validate known-contract negatives as correct-already, harmful, irrelevant, unsupported or incompletely repairable. Unsupported source/effects receive independently reviewed structural-refusal labels and are not executed to manufacture a task score; the same restriction applies during custody validation. **Gate 2 supports keeping the record approach only if all conditions hold:**

- Added C-specific implementation/review effort remains within one engineer-day beyond the common B pipeline; if a simpler ordinary baseline rule offers the same mechanism at lower cost, do not declare richer machinery necessary.

Keep B if it meets the shared observation/safety qualification; if neither does, return to the smallest failed boundary. ## 9. Future autonomous acquisition interface

**UNTESTED HYPOTHESIS:** a fixed inert representation can later accept learned records without rewriting the interpreter. Minimum fields:

`record_schema, record_id, version, origin{ENGINEERED|ACQUIRED}, interpreter_version, operator_ids, applicability_predicates[], binding_roles[], proposal_priority_relation, transformation_obligations[], required_postconditions[], counterexample_refs[], unsupported_conditions[], unknown_fields[], evidence_refs[{artifact_hash, task_contract_hash, source_hash, patch_hash, checker_hash, domain_id, outcome, role}], supersedes`.

Predicates are enumerated role/relationship atoms—known ordering, rank orientation, return dependency, unique definition, ownership—not executable Python, arbitrary prose commands or a general expression evaluator. Operations are fixed single-site sign toggles. Record postconditions reference obligations already demanded by task authority or add caution; they cannot delete or redefine those obligations. Evidence refs are controller-resolved IDs, not arbitrary paths. Unknown is a first-class value, not false or permission. Record confidence alone has no authorizing effect.

The proposed minimal vocabulary is precise enough to bound the future interpreter: roles `SCORE_FIELD`, `TAG_FIELD`, `RANK_MAP`, `RANK_LOOKUP`, `KEY_COMPONENT`, `SORT_CONTAINER`, `RETURN_SELECTOR`; facts `UNIQUE_DEFINITION`, `RETURN_DEPENDENT`, `KNOWN_SORT_BUILTIN`, `OWNED_OUTER_LIST` are three-valued; `SORT_DIRECTION` is ascending/descending/unknown; `SELECTION_END` is first/last/unknown; `MAP_ORIENTATION` relative to current tag order is aligned/reversed/nonmonotone/unknown; `CURRENT_SIGN` is positive/negative/unknown. A clause is at most eight `(role, fact, enum_value)` equality atoms joined by conjunction, one `TOGGLE_SIGN` proposal operation and an integer priority tier 0–3. At most eight clauses; no negated unknown, arbitrary arithmetic, quantifiers, recursion, string execution or task-ID/hash matching. Unknown prevents that clause from prioritizing; ties fall back to B's order. This small table of conditional priorities is the entire additional interpreter initially proposed, not a general contract language.

Required postconditions are references to the common obligation IDs `EXACT_WINNER`, `EXACT_RETURN_TYPE`, `INPUT_VALUE_PRESERVED`, `NO_UNAUTHORIZED_EFFECT`, `LIVE_SINGLE_SITE`; a record cannot assert they passed. The controller checks them independently. If these atoms cannot express a needed hypothesis, record `UNSUPPORTED_HYPOTHESIS`; do not add an interpreter branch during acquisition or after scored outcomes. This expressiveness limit is itself falsifiable. Generic B may use the same extracted facts; only the engineered conditional priority table is withheld. Any common extractor already encoding the whole repair solution must be disclosed as engineered scaffolding rather than credited to the record.

The learner would need to acquire relations connecting implementation context, repair and verified outcome; failure contexts and counterexamples; and limits on useful reuse. Merely copying today's tag order or ownership requirement into a record is not acquired cross-task competence. Independently verified failed reuse may support a candidate revision, but unrelated errors, infrastructure failures and uncertain feedback cannot become harmful-transfer labels. Versions must preserve contradictory evidence and replay retained positives as well as negatives. Acquisition cannot alter the interpreter, authority registry or checker.

A future acquisition gate must compare a verified **autonomously acquired** record with the same capable local system lacking that record, with identical current task facts, model artifact, prompts, tools, verification and budget. The no-record arm may reason out the same relation natively. If it does, there is no retained-state gain under that budget. Account for acquisition/validation cost as well as later reuse; no claim of autonomous learning from C's engineered success. VSL can store versions/evidence after a separately qualified schema migration, but its existing lifecycle labels and caller-provided evidence strings do not certify semantic truth.

## 12. Shortest staged path and final questions

1. **Design gate, now:** independent human review of this package, assign custody and approve baseline/authority/observations. Any critical unresolved requirement ⇒ HOLD. No implementation by this report.
2. **Engineered-value gate:** implement only the bounded shared B/C vehicle after explicit approval; qualify public infrastructure controls; freeze; independently instantiate and run the sixteen-unit screen once. 3. **Acquisition gate, only if justified:** freeze interpreter; let a bounded local learner propose records from permitted verified successes/counterexamples. Require genuine acquired content, traceable feedback and equal-budget no-record control. Manual semantic answers invalidate an acquisition claim.
4. **Transfer and persistence:** independently selected new tasks with acquired-record availability as the sole intended difference; fresh-process reload and ablation. Positive engineered C results are not substitutes.
5. **Net utility:** new natural workload selected independently of skill applicability, same capable local system with/without learned records, count every harm, abstention and cost. Only that evidence can support “experience makes future Echo better.” Do not reuse the October 7 exam for development or scoring.

**Q1. Can a cheaper same-verifier proposer deliver most benefit?** Yes, plausibly; untested. Much of the safety benefit may be common verification, and this defect family admits cheap generic edits. B is the default if C cannot beat it by the declared practical margin.

**Q2. What extra information does a record add?** A cross-task applicability/polarity relation, operand-role restrictions and known counterexample limits used to prioritize a fixed edit universe. No extra current-task authority. Paired record/no-record input isolates access to this engineered information; it does not isolate storage format from information content.

**Q3. What prevents false confidence from a wrong contract?** Independent owner provenance, reviewer translation checks, immutable source/revision binding, conflict/unknown rejection and a separate oracle. Nothing guarantees true intent when all authoritative inputs are wrong. Report conditional conformance and abstain when intent is unavailable.

**Q4. Which observations suffice?** Exact tagged return and final primitive input values on declared sampled inputs, combined with a qualified closed grammar/effect checker. General identity, arbitrary aliases, hidden/global/transient effects, unsupported Python and unseen-input correctness remain unverified; reject requirements needing them.

**Q5. Is genuine holdout separation established?** No. A feasible offline/separate-identity plan is specified, but no custodian, reviewer or access proof exists. This is a present HOLD condition, not a claim of blindness.

**Q6. What would justify richer machinery?** Complete sixteen-unit results meeting ≥6/8 coverage, all strata, at least two extra correct repairs over competent B with no lost B successes, zero unsafe/irrelevant C acceptance, valid refusal, matched budgets and declared cost limits, followed by independent cost/alternative review. It justifies a bounded acquisition test, not deployment.

**Q7. What would justify choosing B?** Tied/lesser correctness, one extra repair only, harmful C acceptance, excessive cost or case-specific patching. If B also fails authority, neither is qualified. A tie is evidence to avoid extra expenditure in this setting, not proof that all semantic representations are useless.

**Q8. Can acquired learning later use the same interpreter?** The proposed inert atom/role/operator interface permits it in principle. Expressiveness and acquisition are untested; a learner must supply new validated data, not require investigator-written interpreter changes for each example.

**Q9. Smallest implementation to authorize?** The shared closed-grammar proposer, one optional inert priority record, authority validator, exact observation adapter, identical independent controller and durable journal with two-room qualification. No general analyzer, new learner, production matcher change or full semantic-contract platform. Authorization awaits review and custody prerequisites.

**Q10. Shortest defensible path to positive retained net utility?** First disprove or establish engineered record value against B; then independently acquire/freeze records, test fresh transfer with an equally capable no-record control, verify reload, and run one new independent natural-workload comparison. Do not advance because a record exists or a patch runs.
