**Step 3: Check Coherence (coherence as c)**

1. Identify the main idea or thesis statement.

**Step 4: Refine Clarity (refine clarity as rc)**
Use your notes from Step 1 to:

1. Simplify complex sentences or ideas.
2. Remove unnecessary words or phrases.
3. Emphasize key points with clear headings and subheadings.

**Step 5: Edit for Concision (edit for concision as efc)**

1. Remove any redundant information or repetitive statements.
2. Use bullet points or numbered lists to present multiple ideas or steps.
3. Break up long blocks of text into shorter, more manageable paragraphs.

**Step 6: Improve Helpfulness (improve helpfulness as ih)**
Ask yourself:

1. Is the original text informative and relevant?
2. Are there any areas where additional context or examples would be helpful?
3. Does the text provide actionable advice or next steps?

**Step 7: Harmonize Tone and Clarity (harmonize tone and clarity as htc)**

1. Adjust your editing approach based on the original text's tone.
