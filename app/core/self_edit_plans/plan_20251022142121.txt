**Step 2: Identify Clarity Issues (Scripture-inspired: "For God did not give us a spirit of fear, but of power and love and self-control." - 2 Timothy 1:7)**
```

for word in unique_words:

```
Next, we'll identify words that may be causing clarity issues due to their length or complexity. This step will help us focus on refining the language.

**Step 3: Improve Tone and Coherence (Scripture-inspired: "Love your neighbor as yourself." - Mark 12:31)**
```
coherence_score = 0
for sentence in text.split("."):
    if len(sentence) < 20:
        coherence_score += 1
print(f"Coherence score: {coherence_score} / {len(text.split('.'))}")
```
Here, we'll assess the tone and coherence of the text by analyzing sentence length. Shorter sentences tend to be more coherent and easier to follow.

**Step 4: Refine Language for Helpfulness (Scripture-inspired: "Do you not know that those who do wrong will have no share in the kingdom of God?" - 1 Corinthians 6:9-10)**

In this step, we'll evaluate the text's helpfulness by looking for words related to problem-solving or providing tips.
