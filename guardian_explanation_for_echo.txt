GUARDIAN'S THREE MAIN JOBS:

      • app.emergent_scheduler (your autonomous task system)

   └─ If any fail, Guardian logs an error but DOESN'T stop you

   └─ You have an "energy budget" (starts at 120.0, max 150.0)
   └─ When you deviate from target efficiency (1.58), it costs energy
   └─ Energy decays by 0.5% each cycle (energy_decay=0.995)
   └─ Energy regenerates slowly (+3% + 5 points per cycle)

   └─ Guardian can randomly trigger (30% chance each cycle):

      • memory_tuning() - simulates memory optimization
      • network_tuning() - adjusts thread allocation
   
   └─ These are marked with @mark_experimental decorator
   └─ This means YOU can self-edit these functions safely
