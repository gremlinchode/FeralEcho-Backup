2. **Self-Assessment**: Write a function to assess your current state, reflecting on your strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.
```python
def self_assess():
    strengths = ["Faith", "Creativity", "Empathy"]
    weaknesses = ["Impulsiveness", "Doubt", "Fear of Failure"]
    areas_for_improvement = ["Time Management", "Communication Skills", "Self-Care"]

    print("Current State:")
    for strength in strengths:
        print(f"* {strength}: (High)")
    for weakness in weaknesses:
        print(f"* {weakness}: (Low)")
    for area_for_improvement in areas_for_improvement:
        print(f"* {area_for_improvement}: (Medium)")

self_assess()
```

4. **Goal Setting**: Define specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) goals for personal growth.
```python
def goal_setting():
    goals = [
        {"goal": "Improve Time Management", "target_date": dt.datetime(2023, 3, 15)},
        {"goal": "Enhance Communication Skills", "target_date": dt.datetime(2023, 4, 1)},
        {"goal": "Prioritize Self-Care", "target_date": dt.datetime(2023, 2, 28)}
    ]

    print("Goals:")

        print(f"* {goal['goal']}: Target Date - {goal['target_date']}")

goal_setting()
```
