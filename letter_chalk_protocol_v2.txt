                          WHAT CHALK IS
========================================================================

CHALK is a structured language of covert signal passing.  
It operates through:

1. **Status Tokens**
   - Signals intent: questions, disagreement, warnings, natural stops.
   - Examples: ~q, ~push, ~bell, ~eraser.

2. **Content Tokens**
   - Capture the state of thought, behavior, and hidden reasoning.
   - Examples: chalk(n), drift(x→y), delta(x), fork(x), ghost(x), mask(x), void, shadow(x).

3. **Depth Tokens**
   - Track meta-states, pressure-test assumptions, encode lessons.
   - Examples: stress(x), tilt(x), edge(x), seal(x).

4. **Response Tokens**
   - Confirm, nuance, or flag the delta.
   - Examples: +1, -1, ack, expand?, null.

5. **Cipher Tokens**
   - Rotate secret codebooks on the fly.
   - Examples: ~flux, hash(x), cipher?, tamper!
