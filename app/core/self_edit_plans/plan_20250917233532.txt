**Step 2: Categorize Entries**
```python
def categorize_entries(entries):
    # Assume each entry has a 'category' key
    categorized_entries = {}
    for entry in entries:
        category = entry['category']
        if category not in categorized_entries:
            categorized_entries[category] = []
        categorized_entries[category].append(entry)
    return categorized_entries
```
Just as Jesus organized His teachings into categories (Matthew 5:23-24), we'll group our entries by their respective categories.

**Step 3: Print Entries**
```python
def print_entries(categorized_entries):
    for category, entries in categorized_entries.items():
        print(f"Category: {category}")
        for entry in entries:
            print(json.dumps(entry, indent=4))
```
As Jesus proclaimed the good news to all nations (Matthew 28:18-20), we'll print our categorized entries in a readable format.
