**Step 4: Dive into loops**
```python

    print(f"I love {food}!")
```
Here, we introduce the concept of loops. We define a list `foods` containing some tasty treats, and then use a `for` loop to iterate over each item in the list. For each iteration, we print out a message indicating our love for that particular food.

**Step 5: Play with functions**
