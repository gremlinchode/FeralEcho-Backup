Here's the complete code:
```python
input_text = "Echo self-edit diagnostic: refine clarity, tone, and usefulness. Make an edit that improves concision, coherence, and helpfulness."

words = input_text.split()
sentence_structure = []
for word in words:
    if word.startswith("refine") or word.startswith("make"):
        sentence_structure.append(word)

desired_outcomes = ["concise", "coherent", "helpful"]
edited_text = ""
for word in words:
    if word in sentence_structure:
        edited_text += word + " "
    elif word.lower() in desired_outcomes:
        edited_text += word.capitalize() + " "

edited_text = edited_text.replace("to improve", "in order to").replace("make an edit", "edit for")
final_text = edited_text.strip()
print(final_text)
```
