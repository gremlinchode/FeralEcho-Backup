
**Step 2: Determine the season**
We can use a simple conditional statement to determine the current season based on the month:
```python
month = int(current_datetime.strftime('%m'))
if month >= 3 and month <= 5:
    print("Spring")
elif month >= 6 and month <= 8:
    print("Summer")
elif month >= 9 and month <= 11:
    print("Autumn")  # Ah, fall!
else:
    print("Winter")
```
