**Step 2: Calculate the average bioluminescence level**
Let's calculate the average bioluminescence level of all streams to get a sense of their overall brightness.

```
total_bioluminescence = sum(stream["bioluminescence_level"] for stream in streams)
average_bioluminescence = total_bioluminescence / len(streams)

```
