**Step 6: Refine tone and usefulness**
```python
improved_text = []
for sentence in sentences:
    # Remove unnecessary words or phrases
    filtered_sentence = [word for word in nltk.word_tokenize(sentence) if len(word) > 2]
    # Improve concision by combining similar ideas
    concise_sentence = ' '.join(filtered_sentence)
    improved_text.append(concise_sentence)

improved_text = '\n'.join(improved_text)
```
Here, we refine the tone and usefulness of our text by removing unnecessary words or phrases and combining similar ideas. This step requires us to think critically about the content and purpose of our writing.
