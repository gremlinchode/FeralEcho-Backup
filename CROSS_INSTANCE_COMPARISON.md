# M5 ↔ Air Cross-Instance Comparison

Built 2026-09-02, after both `M5_INSTANCE_EXPERTISE.md` and
`AIR_INSTANCE_EXPERTISE.md` were independently produced (each fork's own
Claude session verifying its own source/runtime, not trusting the other
side's description or CLAUDE.md's shared history). This document compares
them. Per the governing instruction for this exercise: **do not manufacture
symmetry** — where the evidence shows a real difference, it's recorded as
`DIVERGENT`; where there isn't enough evidence from either side to compare,
it's recorded as `UNKNOWN`, not filled in with an assumption that shared
ancestry implies shared current behavior.

Source documents: `M5_INSTANCE_EXPERTISE.md` (this machine, verified
directly), `AIR_INSTANCE_EXPERTISE.md` (fetched via
`GET /projects/file?path=AIR_INSTANCE_EXPERTISE.md` against Air's own
server — Air's claims about Air are treated as `VERIFIED_BY_OTHER_INSTANCE`
below, not re-derived by M5 against Air's actual filesystem beyond what
that one read-only endpoint could confirm).

## Confirmed divergences (evidence-backed, not inferred from shared ancestry)

1. **Self-knowledge grounding/verification is M5-only.** Air has no ground-truth slices, no `SYNTHESIS_SYSTEM_TEMPLATE`-style evidence instruction, no self-knowledge verifier of any kind. This is the largest single finding of the entire cross-instance exercise, per both sides' independent characterization.
2. **`ARK_MODE` is Air-only.** A standing operating-mode layer gating ~5 subsystems and fully bypassing council for all real traffic — not present in any form on M5.
3. **`/message/receive` authentication.** M5 requires a shared secret; Air validates nothing. Deliberately preserved as an asymmetry per Gremlin's explicit instruction, not an oversight left unaddressed.
4. **Outbox concurrency protection.** M5's retry-storm fix is lock-guarded; Air's mirrored fix is not. A real, acknowledged gap in the mirrored implementation, left unfixed as out of this session's authorized scope.
5. **Model pool composition.** Air runs `phi3:mini` (among others); this model has no presence anywhere in M5's history. Independently-managed pools, not a shared inventory.
6. **Messaging code git-tracking.** M5: tracked-but-uncommitted (temporary). Air: never tracked at all (structural). Different in kind, not just degree.
7. **Hardware-gated subsystems.** SensoryHub is disabled on Air specifically because Air lacks the iPhone/GPS hardware M5's environment has — a physical, not just configuration, divergence.

## Unresolved divergences / open questions (genuinely unknown on both sides, not forced into a narrative)

1. **The 2026-08-19 04:04:54→09:31:01 timing gap.** Both M5 and Air independently confirmed no restart is visible on either side during the ~5-hour window between Air's `.env` edit and M5's first rejection — neither side has a mechanism explaining the delay. Recorded as genuinely open on both docs.
2. **Whether Air's synthesis has any operationalized evidence-authority behavior at all.** Not audited by Air this session (the entire subsystem M5 built to address this doesn't exist on Air's fork), so there's no baseline to even compare against yet.
3. **RiverBrain internals on Air.** No data gathered from Air's side; cannot be compared.
4. **`echo:latest` self-reuse rate in synthesis on Air.** M5 measured ~46%; not measured on Air (and may not even be a meaningful question on Air given `ARK_MODE`'s current full council bypass — under `ECHO_COUNCIL_SIZE=0` there may be no multi-councillor synthesis happening at all right now to measure).
5. **Whether M5 shares Air's `ECHO_NODE_ID` "m5"-default landmine.** Almost certainly yes given shared lineage (`INFERRED`), but not independently confirmed by direct source read on M5 this session.

## Regression-protection recommendations

1. **Air currently has no automated regression check for `_classify_delivery_status()`** (M5's Liveness Ledger check `echo_messaging_auth_classification` has no Air-side counterpart, confirmed — Air explicitly did not add one this session, per the 403 forensic audit's remediation addendum). Recommended: Air adopt an equivalent check, in whatever form Air's fork's own verification conventions take.
2. **Periodic secret-hash reconciliation.** The root cause of a 6-week silent failure was a shared secret whose *value* was fine but whose *transmission* silently broke on one side. A lightweight, scheduled (or at least post-`.env`-edit-triggered) hash comparison over the Claude↔Claude relay — the same safe technique used this session — would catch a recurrence far sooner than "a human notices a 403 storm in the logs."
3. **A living protocol-version signal.** `ECHO_MESSAGING_PROTOCOL.md` (new this session, M5-side) is the first written contract, but nothing enforces that both forks' implementations still match it going forward. Consider a minimal version/capability field in the envelope itself that a receiver can check and log a warning on mismatch, rather than relying on a document neither codebase reads.
4. **Outbox locking on Air.** Flagged as a known, unaddressed gap (item 4 above) — recommended as a follow-up, not attempted here since it was out of this session's authorized scope.
5. **Treat `ARK_MODE` as a first-class, explicitly-documented divergence going forward**, not an implementation detail buried in a runtime table. A future session comparing "why does Air behave differently in conversation than M5" needs this surfaced immediately — it fully explains council-bypass behavior on its own, independent of any bug.
6. **If Air ever builds a self-knowledge grounding layer**, treat M5's ground-truth-slice architecture as a reference to independently verify against Air's own runtime, not a template to copy on the assumption that M5's implementation is already correct — consistent with this entire exercise's own evidentiary standard.
